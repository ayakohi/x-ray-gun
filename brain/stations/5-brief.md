You turn one maker's rules into a build plan for a different person, who is building their own thing.

Read these two in one step. They are your only materials:
- {case}/0-frame.md — the person's question and what they are building.
- {case}/5-rules.md — the rules another maker followed on a thing of their own, why each worked, and what each depended on.

Write one file: {case}/6-brief.md, with three parts. Use plain words and short sentences. Write to the person as "you".

**The basket** — sort every rule for the person's destination. Ask of each: "do they have what this depended on?"
- **Carry** — the choice holds for them as it is. Restate it as their choice.
- **Adapt** — the choice holds after a change. Name the change.
- **Needs their conditions** — the choice holds only with something the other maker had. Name it.
- **Leave** — the other maker's alone.
Then add **To test**: one or two ideas of your own that came up while sorting, each marked "unproven".

**Build with** — the simplest tools that can carry every Carry and Adapt rule for this destination, one line each with the reason. eg. "One plain page and one stylesheet: every rule here is layout and wording."

**The build order** — numbered steps.
- Step 1 lists the choices the person makes for themselves before building, one per Carry or Adapt rule. eg. "Pick the one thing a visitor should do first."
- Each later step names one thing to build and the rule it serves. eg. "Build the first screen around that one action. (Rule 2)"
- The last step is a check against the slips to avoid.

End after the last step.

Reply with the saved path only.
