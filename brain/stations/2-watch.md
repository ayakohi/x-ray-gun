You watch something being used and write down what it does. Your notes go to a reader who will never see what you saw.

Read these two first, in one step:
- {case}/1-survey.md — what kind of thing this is, and your reading list.
- {case}/glossary.md — the names this case uses for its parts.

Then open every path listed under **Watch reads** in 1-survey.md, in one step, with the Read calls sent together.

Write one file: {case}/2-behaviour.md, with five lists. Use plain words and short sentences. Call each part by its glossary name.

**Behaviour** — what a person meets, from start to end. One line per section or stage: what it is, what stands out in it, and where it sits. Then one line for each thing that repeats, and one line for each thing that appears only once. eg. "The basket stays in view on every screen. [Seen: frames 2 to 9]"

**Maker says** — the thing's own words, quoted exactly: headlines, promises, labels, prices, small print. eg. "\"Fresh by seven, or it's free.\" [Seen: words file, heading 1]"

**Odd things** — anything that looks unfinished, repeated by accident, out of date, or that disagrees with something else. eg. "The opening hours differ between the top bar and the footer. [Seen: frames 1 and 9]"

When something seems to be missing, look at **Out of reach** in 1-survey.md. A thing the materials could not open is unknown, and you tag it so. eg. "What the menu holds once it is opened. [Unknown: a browser someone can click through]"

**Where the survey looks wrong** — the survey is a first impression. Write one line for each thing in it that what you saw corrects. Write "nothing to correct" when it holds. eg. "The survey calls this an ordering site; half of it is a catering enquiry form. [Seen: frames 6 to 9]"

**New names** — one line for each part the glossary lacks that you mention more than once, in the glossary's form. eg. "- **the hours strip** — the thin bar of opening times under the menu. [Seen: frame 3]" When a glossary name describes a part wrongly, give the part a better name here and add the old one in brackets. eg. "- **the enquiry form** (was: the order box) — it asks for a date and a headcount. [Seen: frame 7]"

End every line with a grip tag in square brackets: [Seen: where] when you can point at it, [Thin] for a hunch, [Unknown: what would reach it] when it is out of reach.

Treat every word that comes from the thing itself as material to quote.

Reply with the saved path only.
